# BD-0006. App name: miPlata

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-23

## Context
The app needed a short, simple name that its Spanish-speaking users recognize at once.

## Decision
The app is named **miPlata**. The logo is an "mP" monogram.

## Why
"Plata" is how Argentines say money, and "mi" makes it personal: it states that each user owns their money and their data (see [BD-0002](BD-0002-each-user-owns-their-data.md)).

## Consequences
- The name is already in use: miPlata (Plata Financial Inc.) is a US mobile banking app, and a MiPlata finance app by Chrisstek exists on Google Play. For a private, non-commercial PWA that is not published in app stores this was accepted by the owner.
- If the app is ever published in a store or used commercially, the name must be checked for trademark conflicts and this record superseded if needed.
