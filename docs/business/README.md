# Business decisions

Each product or business decision gets one file: `BD-NNNN-short-title.md`, numbered in order and never renumbered. The goal is to remember why the product works the way it does. A decision that changes is not edited: a new record supersedes it.

## Template

```markdown
# BD-NNNN. Title

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by BD-NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context
The need or situation behind the decision.

## Decision
What the product does.

## Why
The reasons, in business terms.

## Consequences
Impact on users, cost, and technical design.
```
