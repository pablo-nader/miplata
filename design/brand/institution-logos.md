# Institution logos

Logos of banks and virtual wallets shown in the account badge ([BD-0012](../../docs/business/BD-0012-institution-logos.md), [ADR 0039](../../docs/adr/0039-institution-logos.md)). Files live in `apps/web/public/institutions/`. They are trademarks of their owners, used only to identify the institution in a private, non-commercial app. The owner approved each source on 2026-11-23.

Licenses are quoted as the source states them. "Public domain" on Wikimedia Commons describes the file (simple text or shapes below the threshold of originality); the trademark still belongs to the institution.
